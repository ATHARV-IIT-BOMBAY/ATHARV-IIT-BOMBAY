I am a **B.Tech. Mechanical Engineering student at IIT Bombay**, pursuing a **minor in Mathematics** and currently working as a **Quant Research Intern at the Quant Finance Institute**.

I build software at the intersection of **quantitative finance, machine learning, and high-performance systems** — from risk engines that explain their numbers to multimodal pipelines, geospatial assistants, and low-latency C++ simulations.

My favourite problems sit where a model meets a real constraint: **latency, uncertainty, reliability, interpretability, or a real person who needs the system to work.**

## What I am building

- **Quantitative finance:** VaR/ES platforms, volatility modelling, backtesting, stress testing, and execution simulations.
- **Machine learning:** classical ML, deep learning foundations, multimodal reasoning, and grounded AI systems.
- **Systems engineering:** C++ performance, producer-consumer IPC, graph optimisation, asynchronous Python, and resilient pipelines.
- **Useful interfaces:** turning complex models into dashboards and tools that people can actually operate.
